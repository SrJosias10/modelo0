ABOUT US

Company Overview.

Our journey began long before JJS Services Inc. was founded.

It started decades ago, when a young Army veteran named Jack left Brooklyn, New York, and made his way to sunny Florida. With determination and grit, Jack became a licensed Florida state contractor, a small business owner, a devoted husband, father and, eventually, a mentor.

One day, Jack crossed paths with a young man named Brad, also from Brooklyn. Brad was eager to learn and soaked up everything Jack had to teach. By then, Jack held multiple state construction licenses and was a respected figure in the South Florida construction scene.

Fast forward 30 years, three companies, and many children and grandchildren later, Brad and Jack remain close friends. Now a 35-year veteran of the construction industry, Brad founded JJS Services Inc. with a bold vision: to make construction fast, affordable, and accessible.

Whether you’re adding an addition to your home, opening a restaurant, or resolving a property violation, JJS Services Inc. was built to be your one-stop solution, from design to final inspection.

The name JJS Services Inc. is a tribute to the man who started it all: Jack Joel Schulman.

Our values are rooted in friendship, mentorship, and family. Brad’s son Trevor now works alongside him, continuing the family tradition and the spirit of good old fashioned hard work.

So if you’re in South Florida and looking for a team you can trust, come visit us. And if you're lucky, you just might hear: “Hey guys, what are we building today?”

What is your process from start to finish?

We start with a free consultation, then submit your plans and permits for city approval. Once approved, construction begins. Throughout the project, we coordinate all required inspections. After the final inspection, we hand the completed project back to you.

How long will my project take?

Project timelines vary depending on the scope of work, permitting and inspections. We can discuss your specific project during your consultation.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

As your contractor, we take care of the permit process and coordinate required inspections.
